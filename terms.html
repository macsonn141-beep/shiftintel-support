ShiftIntel — Use & Purchase Information

Last updated: 5 October 2026 · Developer: Lian Chong Nui

Pre-release information. ShiftIntel v1 is in development. Product details below describe the planned launch and will be verified before release.

The license for the App Store application is Apple’s Licensed Application End User License Agreement. This page explains the planned product scope and purchase options; it does not replace that license.

Estimates and decisions

ShiftIntel’s planned estimates use the schedule and pay rules you enter. A calculated non-work interval is a time calculation, not a fitness-for-duty or recovery assessment. Expected-versus-actual pay differences identify items to verify; they are not legal findings about wages owed.

Your records

You are responsible for the accuracy of your inputs and for keeping copies of records you want to retain. The planned app keeps Baseline rotation versions and Actual work separate. Exporting or sharing a file will be your choice. The locked v1 design excludes employer and payroll integrations and a developer account backup service.

Free and Pro

The planned free tier includes one active job, one rotation, the baseline calendar, basic Actual logging, a basic current pay-period estimate, a next-shift reminder, local storage and exportable data. Pro is planned to unlock up to five active jobs and the advanced pay, rotation, pickup, reconciliation, rest-window, history and export tools.

Expired or removed Pro entitlement must not delete existing data. Existing records will remain exportable; creating or editing restricted features may require valid entitlement.

Planned US launch purchases

Purchases are not available through this site. These are the locked US launch decisions. Before any purchase, the app will show the localized price returned by StoreKit and Apple’s purchase sheet will show the actual price, duration and eligibility.
