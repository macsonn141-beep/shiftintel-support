ShiftIntel Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026 · Developer: Lian Chong Nui

Pre-release information. ShiftIntel v1 is in development. Product details below describe the planned launch and will be verified before release.

This policy describes the planned privacy practices for ShiftIntel v1. The app is in development; the final app and its privacy disclosures will be checked against these practices before release.

Your work and pay records

The planned app will store and process the jobs, rotation versions, actual shifts, pay rules, pay records and notes you enter locally on your device. We do not plan a ShiftIntel account system or backend for these records. The app will not upload them to us, provide app-level cloud synchronization, sell them or use them for advertising or tracking. The locked v1 design excludes third-party analytics, external AI and location tracking.

Exports and device backups

You will decide when to export CSV or JSON records or share a file. Your chosen recipient or service will receive that file and handle it under its own terms. Device and operating-system backup settings may separately affect backups of local app data. Keep exported backups somewhere you trust; the developer cannot recover a private device database we never receive.

Purchases

The planned app will use Apple’s App Store and StoreKit for payment, purchase verification and restoring Pro access. We will not receive your payment-card details. Apple’s handling of purchases and Apple Account information is governed by Apple’s policies.

Notifications

Shift reminders are planned as local notifications. You will be able to grant or withdraw notification permission in your device settings.

Support messages

If you choose to email us, we receive the email address and information you send for that support request. We use this voluntarily supplied correspondence to respond to and resolve the request. Support email is separate from the app’s local database. Please redact employer-sensitive information and pay details, and do not send an entire backup unless needed.

Visiting these pages

These static pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising or tracking code. When a page is hosted, its hosting provider may process standard connection information to deliver it. Email links open your chosen email application; external links are subject to the destination’s practices.

Your control

The planned app will let you control the records you enter and the files you share. Export records before removing data you want to keep. Device backups and copies already shared may remain outside the app. Loss of Pro entitlement must not delete existing records, and existing data will remain exportable.

Updates and contact

We will update this policy if the app’s practices change. For privacy questions, email macsonn141@gmail.com with the subject “ShiftIntel Privacy”.

ShiftIntel provides estimates based on the schedule and pay rules you enter. It is not payroll, tax, legal, medical, fatigue, or workplace-safety advice. Verify pay, policy, and work requirements with your employer or relevant professional source.
