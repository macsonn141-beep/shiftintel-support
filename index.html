ShiftIntel — Privacy & Support

Last updated: 5 October 2026 · Developer: Lian Chong Nui

Pre-release information. ShiftIntel v1 is in development. Product details below describe the planned launch and will be verified before release.

ShiftIntel is being developed for people with complex shifts and pay rules. Its planned core loop compares a baseline rotation with actual work, explains a pickup’s gross-pay impact and the time until the next shift, and helps you compare expected with recorded actual pay.

Privacy and help

  • Privacy policy — planned local data handling, exports, purchases and voluntary support messages.
  • Support — contact the developer and find guidance for the planned launch.
  • Use and purchase information — estimates, Pro options and Apple’s standard application license.
