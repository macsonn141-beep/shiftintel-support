ShiftIntel Support

Last updated: 5 October 2026 · Developer: Lian Chong Nui

Pre-release information. ShiftIntel v1 is in development. Product details below describe the planned launch and will be verified before release.

Contact the developer

For pre-release questions, contact Lian Chong Nui at macsonn141@gmail.com. Use the subject “ShiftIntel Support”. After the app is available, include the app version, iOS/iPadOS version, device model, what you expected and what happened. Screenshots may help; redact names, employer details and pay information. Do not email your Apple Account password or payment details.

Understanding a pay estimate

ShiftIntel’s planned forecasts will use the schedule and pay rules you enter. Check the job timezone, base rate, pay-period anchor, overtime thresholds, breaks or paid-minute overrides, differentials and bonuses. Compare the calculation explanation with those inputs. Verify employer policy and payroll records with the relevant source.

Baseline and Actual

The planned Rotation Twin keeps a versioned Baseline separate from Actual work. Accepting a pickup must create an Actual exception without rewriting the Baseline. A change to the regular rotation must create an effective rotation version that preserves history.

Purchases and restore

At launch, Settings will include purchase and Restore Purchases controls. Restore using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Manage subscriptions through Apple’s subscription settings. Apple handles payment and refund requests. These instructions will be checked against the released app before launch.

Keeping your records

CSV and JSON export are planned in Settings. Export your records before deleting local data or removing the app. ShiftIntel v1 will not provide a developer account or cloud database from which support can recover your schedule and pay records.

ShiftIntel provides estimates based on the schedule and pay rules you enter. It is not payroll, tax, legal, medical, fatigue, or workplace-safety advice. Verify pay, policy, and work requirements with your employer or relevant professional source.
